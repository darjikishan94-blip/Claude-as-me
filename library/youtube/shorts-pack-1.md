## Video 2: Speed vs velocity in 45 seconds
**Title:** Speed vs Velocity: the 45-second difference #shorts
**On-screen hook:** "Same speed. Different velocity?!"
**Script:**
"Two cars, both at 60 km/h. Same speed, right? Yes. Same velocity? Not if one is going north and the other south!
Speed is how fast. Velocity is how fast **and in which direction**.
Here's the trick question. I run one full round of a 400 m track in 80 seconds. My average speed? 400 ÷ 80 = 5 m/s.
My average velocity? I'm back where I started, so my displacement is zero. Velocity = zero!
So, speed: distance ÷ time. Velocity: displacement ÷ time.
Comment 'V' if you'd got that trick question wrong before 😄"
**Text on screen:** Speed = distance/time · Velocity = displacement/time · Round trip → velocity 0
**Tags:** #class9science #motion #physics

## Video 3: 3 diagram mistakes that cost marks
**Title:** 3 Diagram Mistakes That Cost You Marks #shorts
**On-screen hook:** "Your diagram is right… but you still lost marks?"
**Script:**
"Three diagram mistakes I see in almost every answer sheet.
One: label lines that cross each other. Examiners hate that. Keep them straight and parallel.
Two: no arrows on rays in light diagrams. Without arrows, a ray diagram is just lines.
Three: drawing in pen. Use a pencil so you can correct it neatly.
Bonus: always write the **title** under the diagram, like 'Human heart: sectional view'.
Fix these three and you'll pick up easy marks in every Science paper.
Which diagram do you find hardest? Tell me below 👇"
**Text on screen:** ❌ crossing labels · ❌ no arrows · ❌ pen · ✅ title
**Tags:** #boardexam #class10science #studytips

## Video 6: Atom vs molecule vs ion in 30 seconds
**Title:** Atom vs Molecule vs Ion in 30 Seconds #shorts
**On-screen hook:** "3 words everyone mixes up"
**Script:**
"Atom: the smallest particle of an element, like one oxygen atom, O.
Molecule: two or more atoms bonded together, like O₂ or H₂O.
Ion: an atom that has gained or lost electrons, so it carries a charge, like Na⁺ or Cl⁻.
Quick test: Is Na⁺ an atom, a molecule or an ion? Ion! It lost one electron.
Save this before your exam 📌"
**Text on screen:** Atom = O · Molecule = O₂, H₂O · Ion = Na⁺, Cl⁻
**Tags:** #class9science #chemistry #atoms

## Video 8: One memory trick for cell organelles
**Title:** Cell Organelles: Never Forget Them Again #shorts
**On-screen hook:** "Think of a cell as a factory 🏭"
**Script:**
"Here's how to remember cell organelles forever: the cell is a factory.
The **nucleus** is the manager's office, where the instructions (DNA) are kept.
**Mitochondria** are the power plant, making energy as ATP.
**Ribosomes** are the workers who make proteins.
The **ER** is the corridors that carry materials around.
The **Golgi apparatus** is the packing and dispatch department.
**Lysosomes** are the cleaning staff: they can even destroy the factory if it's damaged. That's why they're called suicide bags!
And the **cell membrane** is the security gate deciding who gets in and out.
Which one did you forget most often? 👇"
**Text on screen:** organelle → factory role, one at a time
**Tags:** #class9science #biology #cell

## Video 10: The question that tricks everyone
**Title:** 99% Get This Speed Question WRONG #shorts
**On-screen hook:** "40 km/h there, 60 km/h back. Average speed?"
**Script:**
"I go to my friend's house at 40 km/h and come back at 60 km/h. Average speed?
Most people say 50. It's NOT 50!
Average speed = total distance ÷ total time. Say the distance is 120 km each way. Going takes 120 ÷ 40 = 3 hours. Coming back takes 120 ÷ 60 = 2 hours.
Total: 240 km in 5 hours = **48 km/h**.
You spend more time at the slower speed, so the average leans towards it.
Did you say 50? Be honest in the comments 😄"
**Text on screen:** ❌ 50 · ✅ 48 · total distance ÷ total time
**Tags:** #maths #physics #class9science
