## Script
**[0:00 Hook]**
"About 90 days to your board exams. Feeling like there's too much Science and too little time? Here's the plan my students follow, week by week, so you finish the syllabus, practise like the real exam, and still have time to fix your mistakes."

**[0:30 The big picture: 3 phases]**
- **Phase 1 (Weeks 1–7):** chapter by chapter, 2 chapters a week, a test every weekend.
- **Phase 2 (Weeks 8–11):** board-style practice with full mocks and section drills.
- **Phase 3 (Weeks 12–13):** final polish. Revision sheets only, no new topics.

**[1:30 Phase 1 in detail]** *(show the table)*
"Week 1: Chemical Reactions + Acids, Bases and Salts. Week 2: Metals + Carbon. Week 3: Life Processes + Control and Coordination. Week 4: Reproduction, Heredity and Our Environment. Week 5: Light + Human Eye. Week 6: Electricity + Magnetic Effects. Week 7 is a **catch-up week**: redo your three weakest chapters."
"Why chemistry first? It's very scoring once your equations are solid, and it builds confidence early."

**[3:30 The daily routine: 1.5 hours]**
- 30 min: revision sheet + textbook
- 40 min: questions without looking at the book
- 10 min: one diagram from memory
- 10 min: write mistakes in your **error log**

**[4:30 The secret weapon: the error log]**
"Every mistake you make goes into one page: what you got wrong, and the correct idea. Read it every Sunday. By February, this page is worth more than any guidebook, because it's made of *your* mistakes."

**[5:30 Phase 2: practise like the real thing]**
"Full 3-hour mock, same pattern as your board: CBSE has 5 sections with case-based and assertion–reason questions; GSEB has an objective section plus 'answer any' choices. Mark it honestly, then fix your top 5 mistakes before the next mock."

**[7:00 Phase 3 + exam-day strategy]**
- Two revision sheets a day. No new topics in the last week.
- About 2 minutes per mark, plus 15 minutes at the end for checking.
- Numericals with units, balanced equations, pencil diagrams with neat labels.
- Sleep 7–8 hours. Seriously.

**[8:30 Close]**
"Want the printable plan with the error-log page? [How to get it: link in description / message us on WhatsApp]. Save this video and come back to it every Sunday. All the best, you've got this 💪"
