## 8. Demo class: plan + follow-up
**45-minute demo plan:**
1. (5 min) Welcome, and ask the child what they find hardest in Science.
2. (20 min) Teach one short concept from their current chapter, the Level Up way: concept → example → they try one.
3. (10 min) A 5-question mini quiz, marked on the spot.
4. (10 min) Talk with the parent: what you noticed, how batches work, fees and timings.

**Follow-up messages:**
- *Same day:*
```
Hi! It was lovely meeting [child] today 🙂 [He/She] picked up [concept] really quickly. Let me know if you have any questions about the batch.
```
- *Day 2–3 (if no reply), once only:*
```
Hi! Just checking if you had any questions about [child]'s admission for [Class X]. The batch starts [date]. Happy to help 🙂
```
- *Day 7, final:*
```
Hi! I'll keep a seat for [child] until [date]. Do let me know either way. No pressure at all 🙂
```
