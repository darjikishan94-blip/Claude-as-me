# Class 10 board season: parent message series (Nov 2026 – Feb 2027)

These messages go with the **90-Day Board Revision Plan**. Each one is ready to paste into WhatsApp. Change only the `[brackets]`.
Group messages never name a student or show marks. Anything about one child goes one-to-one.
For the 9th Foundation group, use the "Respected Parents" style instead. These messages are for Class 10.

### 1. Kick-off
```
Hello Parents,
Board exams are about 3 months away, and we're starting our 90-day Science revision plan on Monday, 9 November 📚
Here's how it works:
• 2 chapters a week, each with a revision sheet and a test
• Weekly tests on [day] at [time]
• 2 full board-pattern mocks in January
I'll share the plan with the students. It would really help if you keep an eye on their daily revision at home. About 1.5 hours of Science a day is enough.
Thank you!
```

### 6. Mock result (one-to-one)
Use the **test-results** skill. Share the marks, and it drafts one message per parent in the right tier: top, steady, or needs support.

### 7. Falling-behind check-in (one-to-one)
```
Hi! Hope you're doing well.
[Name] has been [genuine positive: regular in class / trying hard] 🙂 In the last few tests, though, [he/she] has been losing marks in [area].
With the boards close, I'd like to give [him/her] some extra practice: [plan, e.g. one worksheet every two days].
Could you check that [he/she] spends 20 minutes on it at home? Happy to talk anytime.
```

### 8. How parents can help
```
Hello Parents,
A few small things that make a big difference in the next few weeks:
• 7–8 hours of sleep. Late-night studying usually backfires
• Phone and social media kept to a minimum during study hours
• Light, home-cooked food and plenty of water
• Please avoid comparing with other children. Encouragement works much better 🙂
The students are working hard. Let's keep them calm and confident.
Thank you!
```
