## The pick: Science for Class 8–10, CBSE + GSEB, with a "new NCERT books" angle
**Why this one fits:**
- **You already make the content.** Every class you teach is a video idea, and every test paper becomes a "top 5 questions" Short.
- **The timing is good.** NCERT's new *Exploration* (Class 9, 2026-27) and *Curiosity* (Class 8) books are new, so there's far less good content for them than for the old books. Early, clear videos on new books can rank.
- **Being local is an edge.** National channels are huge but rarely cover GSEB and CBSE side by side. Parents in your area can find you, which helps Level Up Academy admissions.
- **You can do it from a phone.** Shorts plus whiteboard or screen-record videos need no studio.

**Main risk:** education YouTube is crowded. You win on *specific* titles (exact chapter, board, year), not generic ones.

### The other two options
| Option | Upside | Downside |
|---|---|---|
| **"Running a tuition class with AI"** (for other teachers and tutors) | Less crowded and trending. You're living it right now | Smaller audience, and doesn't bring local admissions |
| **Study guidance for parents** (board exam strategy, streams after 10th) | Builds trust with local parents, so admissions | Slower growth, and you'd need to be on camera more |

You can add one of these later as a playlist once the main channel is running.

## Cadence you can sustain
- **2 Shorts a week:** 30–60 sec, one concept or one trick or one common mistake.
- **1 long video a week:** 8–15 min chapter explainer or exam strategy.
- Batch-record on one day, schedule the uploads, and reply to comments in the evening.
